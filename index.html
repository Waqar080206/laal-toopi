The Problem

A Familiar Voice Is No Longer Proof.

02 / 14
Illustrative
Illustrative scenario · not a real case

A finance officer receives a voice note that sounds exactly like the company's CEO.

The message requests an urgent transfer to a new beneficiary. A convincing video appears to confirm the instruction. The sender discourages a callback, claiming to be unavailable.

But the real executive never authorised the transaction.

01 · FAMILIARITY

Familiarity creates trust.

Employees naturally recognise voices, faces and communication patterns. Attackers exploit this familiarity.

02 · URGENCY

Urgency suppresses verification.

Time pressure, authority claims, secrecy and callback avoidance encourage employees to bypass normal controls.

03 · DETECTION

Detection does not equal prevention.

A media detector can surface signs of manipulation, but its score alone cannot establish whether the financial instruction is legitimate.

Harm
Fraudulent transfers Unauthorised beneficiary changes Difficult recovery Operational & reputational damage

The vulnerability is not simply a fake voice. It is the gap between recognising someone and verifying that they authorised an instruction.

03 VERIFY

Route to trust

Differentiation

Conventional media analysis focuses on whether content appears manipulated. TRUVY connects media analysis to an operational decision and a verification process · it does not rely on a single detector score.

TRUVY helps answer not only whether media looks suspicious, but what should happen before the associated payment proceeds.

Privacy, Security & Safety

Trust Requires More Than Detection.

07 / 14
Constraints
Privacy
  • Prototype uses synthetic, public or consented data
  • No customer credentials
  • No voiceprint database
  • Ephemeral media processing where feasible
  • Defined retention for escalated evidence
Security
  • Role-based access
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Tamper-evident audit logs
  • Rate limiting
  • Deployment within the institution's security boundary
Human control
  • No automatic payment rejection
  • No fraud accusation from one weak signal
  • Human review for high-risk cases
  • Documented reviewer overrides
  • Dual approval where required

False positive

Verification or review introduces friction, but TRUVY does not independently reject the payment.

False negative

Independent payment-context rules may still trigger verification even when the media detector fails.

TRUVY is designed to support safer decisions even when its media detector is imperfect.
